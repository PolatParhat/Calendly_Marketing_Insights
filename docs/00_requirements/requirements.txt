Step 4: Build the Data Pipeline Define and Calculate Metrics as per Business Requirement: Metrics 1.1 Daily Calls Booked by Source 
● Goal: Count of Calendly bookings per source, per day 1.2 Analyze Cost Per Booking(CPB) by Channel 
● Goal : Determine how much it costs to acquire a lead in each marketing channel. 
● Why it matters : Identifies the most cost-efficient acquisition channels. 
● How to do it : 
○ Formula: Cost per Lead = Total Spend / Total Booked CAlls 
○ Group by channel, then compute cost per lead. 1.3 Bookings Trend Over Time 
● Goal : Track daily or weekly volume of Calendly bookings per source. 
● Why it matters : Spot trends like campaign fatigue, seasonal changes, or the impact of new creatives. 
● How : Group bookings by booking_date and source, then plot counts over time (line chart). 1.4 Channel Attribution 
● Goal : Attribute bookings to specific marketing channels or campaigns using UTM parameters. 
● Why it matters : Identifies which sources (Google, Facebook, YouTube, etc.) and campaigns generate the most and cheapest calls. 
● How : Group bookings by source, and calculate: 
○ total bookings 
○ total spend 
○ CPB Then sort and visualize in a leaderboard or heatmap. 1.5 Booking Volume by Time Slot / Day of Week 
● Goal : Understand when leads prefer booking calls.

=== Page 8 ===
● Why it matters : Helps with resource planning (e.g., setter/closer availability) and scheduling ad delivery (e.g., increasing bids near high-volume slots). 
● How : Extract hour and day_of_week from Calendly booking timestamps and plot heatmaps or histograms grouped by time and channel. 1.6 Understand Meeting Load per Employee 
● Goal : Calculate how many meetings each employee attends weekly. 
● Why it matters : Identifies potential burnout or over-scheduling. 
● How to do it: 
○ Formula: Avg Meetings per Week = Total Meetings / Number of Weeks 
○ Group by employee_id
